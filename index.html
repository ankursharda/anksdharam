About Anks Dharam — Epic AI Films

Anks Dharam is a digital cinematic platform dedicated to bringing Hindu mythology, Sanatan Dharma, Hindu Gods and Goddesses, and ancient Vedic stories to life through original AI-animated Hindu films, cinematic storytelling, visual effects, and immersive sound design.

Created by Ankur Sharda, Anks Dharam explores the timeless wisdom of Sanatana Dharma (Sanatan Dharma) through epic AI films inspired by Hindu scriptures, sacred traditions, and the extraordinary stories of Hindu deities.

Our cinematic universe features Lord Vishnu and his Dashavatar (Ten Avatars), including Matsya Avatar, Kurma Avatar, Varaha Avatar, Narasimha Avatar, Vamana Avatar, Parashurama Avatar, Lord Rama, Lord Krishna, Buddha, and the future Kalki Avatar. Through our Dashavatar series and futuristic Kalki Avatar AI film series, we combine ancient Hindu mythology with modern cinematic technology.

Anks Dharam also presents stories of Lord Shiva (Mahadev), Lord Hanuman, Shani Dev, Goddess Durga, Goddess Lakshmi, and other revered Hindu Gods and Goddesses. Our content includes epic Hindu mythology films, animated devotional stories, Hindu scripture explanations, spiritual documentaries, bhajans, stotrams, aartis, and powerful Hindu mantras.

Our mission is to make the stories, teachings, and cultural heritage of Hindu Dharma accessible to audiences around the world through engaging AI-generated visuals, original storytelling, and cinematic filmmaking.
